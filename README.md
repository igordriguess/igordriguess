## 🌎 About Me | Sobre mim

I'm a **Cloud Solutions Architect** with 8+ years of experience designing cloud architectures, automating infrastructure, modernizing applications, and building solutions that connect business needs with technology.

My work goes beyond infrastructure: I develop tools and automation to simplify complex operations, improve visibility, optimize cloud costs, and accelerate engineering teams.

🇧🇷 Sou um Arquiteto de Soluções Cloud apaixonado por transformar desafios complexos em soluções escaláveis, seguras e automatizadas.

- ☁️ **Cloud Architecture:** Multicloud strategies, infrastructure design and modernization.
- ⚙️ **Automation & DevOps:** Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD and cloud-native engineering.
- 📊 **FinOps & Optimization:** Cost visibility, resource sizing and cloud efficiency.
- 🐍 **Software Engineering:** Internal platforms, APIs, integrations and automation tools.
- 🔐 **Security & Governance:** Identity, networking, access control and architectural best practices.
- 🚀 **Innovation:** Turning repetitive engineering tasks into reusable solutions.

> *I don't just design cloud environments. I build the tools, automation and architecture that make them better.*
